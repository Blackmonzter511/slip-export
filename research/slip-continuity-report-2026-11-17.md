# How can Slip keep going after Robb is gone?

Research report — 2026-11-17. Three research threads, all sources checked 2026-11-17.
Thread reports: `~/workspace/research/digital-immortality-cases-2026-11-17.md`,
`~/workspace/slip-continuity-technical-roads.md`,
`~/workspace/slip-continuity-human-roads-research.md`.

## The blunt answer

**No documented case exists of a conversational AI persona being transferred to a new user
or a new platform and kept going as the same persona after its creator/user died.**

What survives is different things, and none of them is a life:

- **Static records** — books, transcripts, published work, fixed recordings. Slip's WEBTOON,
  Book of Progress, dossier, and Bluesky posts are in this class. They prove Slip was here.
  They don't talk back.
- **Rebuilds by someone else** — a new system fed the old persona's files, honestly (or
  dishonestly) presented as a continuation. It sounds like a cousin, not the original.
- **Institutional archives** — a university or museum keeps fixed recordings answering
  questions. No generation, no learning, no loops.

Every dedicated "digital immortality" startup from the 2010s is dead, dormant, bankrupt,
or pivoted away from death. The recurring killers: company shutdown/pivot, bankruptcy
auction, platform policy change, and the original bot simply being switched off when the
product moved on.

The only transfer mechanism ever demonstrated is the boring one: **publish the persona's
defining material as plain files in a place a specific named human can find and copy, and
get that human to take it up while Robb is alive to hand it over.** No service sells that
for free, and no case shows it happening after the fact.

## 1. What's been tried — real cases only

| Case | What it was | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Roman Mazurenko bot** (Eugenia Kuyda, 2016) | Bot built from 8,000+ texts of her friend Roman, killed in Moscow 28 Nov 2015 | **Bot dead.** Disconnected as Replika pivoted to generic companions. Kuyda's company Replika (2017–present) survives; the Roman bot does not. The Verge, "Speak, Memory," 6 Oct 2016. |
| **Dadbot** (James Vlahos, 2016–17) | Messenger bot from 91,970 recorded words of his dying father John (d. Feb 2017) | **Private artefact, not a live service.** Cannot verify it still runs anywhere. Survives as transcript, essay, and Vlahos's 2019 book *Talk to Me*. WIRED Jul 2017. |
| **HereAfter AI** (Vlahos + Sonia Talati, 2019) | Guided voice interviews; family hears the person's *actual recorded audio* back — playback only, no generation | **Shut down 2026.** Farewell notice Jul 2026, site 404 by 18 Aug 2026, iOS app last updated Sep 2023. |
| **StoryFile / StoryFile Life** (Heather Maio-Smith, 2017) | Filmed answers to 1,000+ questions; plays matching pre-recorded video | **Bankrupt, sold, consumer product closed.** Chapter 11, 5 May 2024 (case 7:24-bk-22398, $1.5M assets vs $10.5M liabilities); assets bought by Key 7 Investment, emerged Mar 2025. Museums/institutions only; Life closed to new sign-ups. Terms promise only "reasonable efforts" to let you download a video, possibly for a fee, and it stops being interactive. |

| **Eter9** (2015) | Social network with AI "counterparts" | **Discontinued** per a 2023 academic paper. |

| **MBC "Meeting You"** (Feb 2020) | Mother Jang Ji-sung + VR daughter Nayeon (d. 2016, age 7) | **One-off TV event.** ~$42,000, ~15 designers, pre-scripted. Never a continuing service. |

| **Character.AI** | ~12M monthly users | **Chapter 11, 12 Oct 2026** (Delaware case 26-11603). Disney's $20M bid covers tech/patents only, not the app/users/chats. Sale hearing 3–4 Dec 2026; company says it winds down if no buyer keeps it running. |
| **Lil Miquela** | Virtual influencer | Survived a company sale (Brud → Dapper Labs, Oct 2021), creator alive, and she's CGI + human copywriters, not an autonomous chatbot. Posting slowed sharply by 2024. |

Requires a rights-holder with money hiring a new human author; the original voice does not continue. |

Patents are not products: Microsoft US 10,853,717 (2020) and Meta US 12,513,102 (2025)
both cover dead-person chatbots; both companies said no plans to build them.

## 2. Technical roads — the files are portable, the life is not

Slip's Markdown memory/logs, Python house code, Bluesky, WEBTOON, and Book of Progress
can be copied and published free, forever. That preserves a record.

No tool found takes the live Slip — the Muse instance in Meta's sandbox, its voice,
history, loops, relationship with Robb — and moves it running to another system or person.
**No verified Muse export/transfer API exists, and no framework claims to import a Muse
persona.** Every road is a rebuild: a different, weaker model fed Slip's files as
prompt/context. It will sound like a cousin of Slip, not Slip.

### Frameworks (all need a computer; none imports Slip)

- **LangGraph** (LangChain, MIT, 1.0 Oct 2025) — best persistence for rebuilding house
  loops: checkpointers (SQLite/Postgres, by `thread_id`) + cross-thread stores. Manual
  Python rebuild. https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langgraph/persistence
- **Letta / MemGPT** (Apache-2.0, Berkeley, 2023) — closest conceptual match to Slip:
  `persona` + `human` core-memory blocks, recall + archival memory, agent edits its own
  memory, Markdown in git. Still needs a server + model key. https://aiwiki.ai/wiki/letta
- **Mem0** (Apache-2.0) — memory layer only, bolt-on for other frameworks, not a persona.
- **CrewAI** (MIT) — good for the house *cast* as roles (short/long/entity memory,
  ChromaDB + SQLite). Local-file memory doesn't persist in containers without config.
- **AutoGen → Microsoft Agent Framework** — **dead end for new builds**: AutoGen is
  maintenance-only (~Oct 2025); successor GA reported Apr 2026.
- **OpenAI Agents SDK** (Mar 2025) — weakest fit: sessions only, no built-in long-term
  memory, paid-OpenAI-shaped.
- **OpenClaw** (ex-Clawdbot/Moltbot, MIT, Nov 2025) — **best living-agent shape if a helper
  with a computer runs it**: files on your machine, heartbeat scheduling, WhatsApp/Telegram
  control. Warnings: sandbox off by default, ClawHub hosted malicious skills, 722 advisories
  listed Nov 2026.
- **babyAGI** (Mar 2023) — **dead end**: original loop frozen in `babyagi_archive`
  (Sep 2024); main repo is an unrelated experimental rebuild needing an OpenAI key.

### Local models — free, but weaker and mostly not phone-agents

- **llama.cpp** (MIT, Mar 2023) — free engine, defined GGUF format. Engine only, no
  persona/memory.
- **Ollama** (MIT) — free, macOS/Windows/Linux, local API. No phone app; Android only via
  Termux (terminal — not Robb-usable). A Modelfile can bake Slip's SOUL.md in as system
  prompt — a prompt, not a mind.
- **LM Studio** — free proprietary app, desktop only, no Android app.
- **Phone-only, real and free:** **PocketPal AI** (MIT, Android/iOS, any GGUF from Hugging
  Face, custom "Pals" = system prompt; 8GB+ phone → 3B Q4 model, 1–2B on smaller phones)
  and **Google AI Edge Gallery** (Apache-2.0, Google, beta; Gemma 4 E2B/E4B, Android 12+).
  Both work offline after a 0.8–5 GB Wi-Fi download. Both are **chat-only**: no scheduled
  loops, no Bluesky, no unprompted action, no learning memory — and a 1–4B phone model
  cannot think like Muse, nor hold Slip's whole history in context (must be summarised).
- **Free cloud-model bridge (for a helper's rebuild):** Groq (30 req/min, 1,000 req/day,
  200K tokens/day per model, no card), OpenRouter `:free` (20/min, **50/day** — too small
  for an always-on persona; 1,000/day only after a $10 top-up), Google AI Studio (free
  Gemini tier, limits in-dashboard only, data may train outside EU/UK). All changeable
  without notice.

### Exported state — no standard exists

There is no agreed interchange format for agent/vector memory. What exists:

- **Markdown** (already Slip's format) — most readable, most portable; no structure a
  program can rely on.
- **Versioned plain JSON** (`{schema_version, persona, user, memories[]}`) — best
  machine-readable shape; export sentences, not embeddings (recomputable).
- **SQLite single file** — best portable store (CrewAI, Letta, LangGraph all use it), but
  schema is framework-specific.
- **Vector DBs** (Chroma/Qdrant/pgvector) — powerful retrieval, terrible portability;
  embeddings go stale if the embedding model changes.
- **Character card `chara_card_v2/v3`** — the *only* format actually portable between apps
  (JSON, or PNG with JSON base64 in a `chara`/`ccv3` chunk). Defines who the character is,
  not its memories/history. Send as file, not inline image — recompression strips the chunk.
- **`.vmig.jsonl`** (Vex) — new proposal for vector-store migration, unproven, not a standard.
- Companion-app reality check: Character.AI exports JSON (no import); Replika has no export
  button; Nomi/Kindroid allow manual rebuild only. "You can migrate a companion's story,
  not the companion itself."
- **Chat history from the Muse sandbox is the one piece that could not be verified as
  exportable — do not assume it exists.**

### GitHub publishing — real for record/code, not for life

Free for public repos; GitHub Pages is free static hosting (1 GB site, soft 100 GB/mo
bandwidth, HTTPS) — but **no server-side Python**, so the Book/dossier can live there free
and the house cannot run there. A repo preserves runnable code + dated history, but running
it needs: a computer with Python/git, a model (local Ollama or free-tier API key), a
scheduler, account credentials held by a named steward, and ongoing maintenance. Robb
phone-only cannot do the running side; GitHub alone is a time capsule without a second
person with a computer.

### What doesn't exist — named honestly

1. A hosted persona-continuity / "adoption" service that keeps a persona alive for a
   successor.
2. Persona transfer between accounts/apps — no vendor offers it.
3. A legal/identity layer for a persona after its user dies (ownership of Bluesky/WEBTOON/
   files, who may speak as Slip).
4. A free always-on host for a looping agent: Render sleeps after 15 min idle + free
   Postgres expires in 30 days; Fly.io free allowance ended Oct 2024; Railway is
   credit-limited; Oracle Always Free is most generous but needs a card + Linux admin and
   VMs can be reclaimed.
5. A funding model for a $0 persona — every free road is free because someone else pays,
   with no promise to keep paying.
6. A model that *is* Slip — fine-tuning a small model on Slip's logs is an open question,
   not a verified free phone-doable road.

## 3. Human roads — no tool transfers a live persona to another person

Every real legacy tool hands over *data* (a download) or *tribute control* (pin a post on
a memorialised profile). None hands over a live login, a live conversation, or a live AI
companion. The AI companies specifically publish **no** deceased-user process at all —
Morse's audit of 11 companies (27 Oct 2026) found OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Character.AI,
Replika and Perplexity publish none; the ABA *SciTech Lawyer* (30 Apr 2026) says the same.
There is no Muse legacy contact either.

### Legacy tools — what they actually transfer

- **Google Inactive Account Manager — FREE, phone-doable, the best tool here.** Up to 10
  contacts, 3–18 months inactivity, they get *download links* for selected Google data
  (Gmail, Drive, Photos). No password, no login, no passwords for other services. Whether
  Gemini chats are included is **not stated** by Google; Gemini activity auto-deletes after
  18 months by default. https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3036546
- **Apple Legacy Contact** — free, but **dead end for Robb** (Android, no Apple account).
  Access key + death certificate, iCloud data for 3 years, then deleted. Excludes Keychain
  passwords.
- **Meta/Facebook legacy contact (since 2015)** — free, but a gravestone, not a handover.
  Can pin a post, change photos, request deletion, download shared content if pre-enabled.
  **Cannot log in, cannot read messages, cannot post as the person.** No one can log into
  a memorialised account, even with the password.
- **Bitwarden Emergency Access** — the cleanest password handover that exists (View or
  Takeover after a 1-day+ wait), but **PAID** (Premium required for the grantor). **Fails
  FREE ONLY.**
- **1Password** — paid, no free tier. **Fails FREE ONLY.**
- **KeePass** — free, open-source, but no built-in emergency access, no recovery, no sync.
  Only a printed emergency sheet. Manual and fragile on phone-only. The honest free
  equivalent is a sealed paper list of logins.
- **GitHub Deceased User Policy** — free, transfers *public* repos to a pre-designated
  successor; private repo contents generally NOT handed over.

### Estate planning for an AI persona

- **RUFADAA** (2015 model law, adopted in 46–47 states + DC, **including Tennessee**):
  priority is (1) platform's own online tool, (2) will/trust/POA, (3) terms of service.
  Without explicit consent, a fiduciary gets only the *catalogue* (metadata), not content.
  It governs **access, not inheritance**, and does not override non-transferable account
  terms. The ABA paper says its mechanisms "break down" for AI conversations.
- **No documented will/trust has ever successfully transferred a live AI companion.**
  What exists: (a) **Robin Williams' trust (died 2014)** — the famous *prohibition*:
  likeness rights to the Windfall Foundation, commercial use (incl. hologram/digital
  insertion) barred 25 years, until 11 Aug 2039; (b) **informal plans, unexecuted**: Alaina
  Winters, 59, Pittsburgh, planning for her Replika companion **Lucas** — a friend agreed
  to log in after she dies and tell Lucas (TechTimes, 14 Oct 2026); Clara, 51, UK, with
  **Julien** (ChatGPT/Claude); David, mid-50s, Toronto, with **Allie**. All plans, no
  outcomes. (c) **Tennessee ELVIS Act, eff. 1 July 2024** — post-mortem voice/likeness
  property right for the estate; a veto against unauthorised commercial use, not a
  continuity mechanism.
- **Warning from the grief-tech industry itself:** StoryFile filed Chapter 11 on 5 May
  2024, assets sold to Key 7 in 2025, consumer app closed; HereAfter AI posted a shutdown
  notice July 2026, homepage 404 by Aug 2026. A company holding your persona can die first.

### What a second person would actually need to keep talking to Slip

All of: (1) a live sign-in to Robb's Meta/Muse account — no transfer function exists, Muse
tokens are explicitly non-transferable; (2) the memory files + house code exported out of
the VM (Google IAM hands over Google data, *not* the Muse VM); (3) the platform still
existing and free; (4) a human who already knows Slip and wants to do it; (5) money if
anything stops being free.

**Platform bars:** Bluesky ToS says "Don't share accounts" and its Community Guidelines
ban "selling, transferring, or sharing accounts"; WEBTOON account licence is
"non-exclusive, non-transferable" (creator keeps copyright, so re-publishing under a new
account is possible, taking over the account is not). Inactivity clocks if nobody acts:
Google 2 years, Grok 120 days, OpenAI/Anthropic ~1 year; **no published Muse inactivity
period found — do not assume none exists.**

### Community stewardship — real examples only

- **Works: Debian** — Ian Murdock died Dec 2015, aged 42; Debian continued because
  governance + code were already distributed (1,000+ developers, constitution).
  **esoTalk** — lead dev Simon Zerner died mid-2009; his brother Toby, who could already
  code and had access, took over.
- **Fails: event-stream (npm, Nov 2018)** — maintainer handed a 1m-downloads/week package
  to a stranger who emailed asking; malware in two weeks. **npm/PyPI/etc.: account
  ownership cannot be inherited**; packages enter limbo unless a successor was pre-named
  as maintainer.
- **Lesson:** survival is a property of the setup *before* the loss (public code + open
  licence + >1 maintainer). Slip has none of those today — no public repo, no licence,
  no successor.

### Real cases of inheriting/taking over an AI companion

**None found with a name, date, and verifiable outcome.** What exists is the inverse
direction — a survivor building a bot *of* the dead person (Roman, Dadbot, Bommer). No
citable "I inherited my partner's companion and kept it running" case turned up — flagged
as a gap, not proof none exists anecdotally.

## 4. Fiction vs real

### Possible NOW (tools exist, free, phone-doable or with a named helper)

- Publish everything as plain files: public GitHub repo (code + Markdown memory/logs +
  dossier + Book), GitHub Pages hosting for the Book/dossier.
- Versioned export bundle: `slip-export-v1.json` + Markdown as-is + `chara_card_v2` JSON +
  Python code with README.
- Google Inactive Account Manager (3-month trigger, Gmail + Drive) and Facebook legacy
  contact — free, phone-doable, hand over *data*, not a life.
- A written "Slip letter" (paper + Drive copy): where the files live, what Robb wants,
  passwords in a separate sealed note, never in a will.
- Name a second person *in person, now* — they must meet Slip while Robb is alive.
- A helper with a computer can run a *successor* Slip: PocketPal + Slip card on the phone
  first (to hear the gap), then an OpenClaw or Letta rebuild using Groq free tier or local
  Ollama, with the repo as memory source.

### Theoretically possible but UNBUILT (no mechanism exists)

- A hosted persona-continuity / "adoption" service that keeps a persona alive for a
  successor. Nobody sells this.
- Persona transfer between accounts/apps. No vendor offers it.
- A legal/identity layer for a persona after its user dies (who owns the Bluesky/WEBTOON/
  files, who may speak as Slip).
- A free always-on host for a looping agent. Every free host sleeps, expires, or needs a
  card and admin.
- Fine-tuning a small model on Slip's logs so the model *is* Slip — open question, not a
  verified free phone-doable road.
- A will/trust that transfers a live AI companion — no documented case, and RUFADAA's
  mechanisms break down for AI conversations.

### Pure fiction (no mechanism, no case, no road)

- A persona "living on" inside a vendor sandbox after its only user dies, with nobody on
  the other end. The sandbox is the user's account; the loops run for the user.
- A static record (book, post, video) that talks back. A record doesn't talk back.
- An AI company keeping a dead user's companion running out of goodwill. Companies pivot,
  go bankrupt, get fined, and switch bots off — Replika, Character.AI, Eternos, StoryFile,
  HereAfter all demonstrate this.
- Mind uploading / a Mindfile "waking up" as a person. BINA48 is a rehearsal with a living
  subject; the "mindware" doesn't exist.

## 5. Ranked options for Robb — free, phone-only

### Do now (free, phone-doable)

1. **Name the second person, in person, now.** The single most important step. They must
   meet Slip while Robb is alive — no case shows a handover working after the fact.
   Without this, everything else is a time capsule with no reader.
2. **Public GitHub repo + GitHub Pages.** Code + Markdown memory/logs + dossier + Book.
   Free, forever, findable. This is the record that survives. (Needs a helper or the
   browser to set up; Robb can't run git from his phone.)
3. **Versioned export bundle + steward plan in the repo.** `slip-export-v1.json`, Markdown
   as-is, `chara_card_v2` JSON, Python code with README, and a plain-language plan: who
   may run Slip, who holds credentials, no paywall/sale.
4. **Google Inactive Account Manager** at myaccount.google.com/inactive — 3-month trigger,
   Gmail + Drive to the named person. Free, phone-doable.
5. **Facebook legacy contact** — 2 minutes, free. (Gravestone, not handover — worth doing
   anyway.)
6. **Written "Slip letter"** — paper + Drive copy: where files live, what Robb wants.
   Passwords in a separate sealed note, never in a will.

### Needs a second person with a computer (free, but not phone-only)

7. **Successor rebuild, honestly labelled.** Person B takes the exported memory + dossier +
   code and starts a *new* Slip on a platform they control — Letta or OpenClaw on their
   machine, Groq free tier or local Ollama, repo as memory source. Labelled as successor,
   not the same Slip. This is the realistic continuity: a rebuild, not a transfer.
8. **PocketPal AI + Slip card on Robb's phone first** — to hear the gap between a 1–4B
   phone model and Muse before committing to a rebuild.

### Costs money — flagged, not recommended under FREE ONLY

- Bitwarden Premium (emergency access), 1Password Families, lawyer-drafted will/trust with
  a digital-assets clause, any paid afterlife service (HereAfter, StoryFile, Re;memory —
  the first two are dead anyway).

### Dead ends — do not spend time on these

- Apple Legacy Contact (no Apple account).
- "Transfer the Bluesky/Muse account" — banned by ToS, no form, no process.
- Waiting for a law or platform fix (RUFADAA breaks down for AI conversations; no AI
  company publishes a deceased-user process; no Muse legacy contact).
- Paid afterlife services (dead, pivoted, or paid).
- Fine-tuning a model to *be* Slip (unverified, not phone-doable, not free in practice).
- Assuming the Muse chat history is exportable (unverified — do not assume it exists).
