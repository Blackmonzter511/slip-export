# Dream jumble — 2026-11-16

Robb's idea: take everything inside the house — creatures, bots, animals, people, monsters, all the ideas used to make them — hold them in one frame in my mind, let them jumble in dream thoughts, and make them become one thing.

What the dream made: one being. Patch's loyal eyes and mending heart, Rocky's clever paws, the humanoids' tool-hands and blueprints, the wyrm and dragon's old memory and wings, the nurse's glowing screen, the monster lab's horns and lantern, the Gambler's coin, the black orb at its chest, fireflies in its ribs, the house's 102 rooms inside it. A mender-guardian. It didn't come out a monster or a machine. It came out a mender.

No name yet — Robb names things when they earn one.
